# Create an account and sign in

The drawing app uses an email address and password. You must verify your email address before the account can be used and before invitations sent to that address can grant access.

## Create an account

1. Open `login.getdraw.app`.
2. Select **Sign up** below the sign-in form.
3. Enter your email address and a password of at least eight characters.
4. Select **Create account**.
5. Open the verification message sent to your email and follow **Verify my email**.
6. After verification, the app signs you in and opens your dashboard.

The verification link expires after 24 hours. If the address already has an account, the app does not reveal that on the sign-up screen; the account owner receives a notice instead.

## Sign in

1. Open `login.getdraw.app`.
2. Enter the verified email address and password.
3. Select **Continue**.

Your session is restored on the same browser until you sign out, change your password, or the session expires. Selecting **Sign out** also clears the active workspace choice on that browser.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and select **Send reset link**.
3. Follow the link in the email.
4. Enter a new password of at least eight characters.

For privacy, the app gives the same confirmation whether or not an account exists for that address. Reset links expire after one hour and can be used only once. Completing a reset invalidates older signed-in sessions.

## Change a known password

From the dashboard, select the gear icon, choose **Password**, and enter the current and new passwords. Changing the password signs out other browser sessions. Approved agent connections remain active until you disconnect them separately under **Agents & MCP**.

## Invitations sent before sign-up

An invitation to a drawing or workspace can be recorded before the recipient has an account. The invitation stays pending until that person creates and verifies an account with the same email address. The app then claims the pending invitation automatically.

## Security notes

- Passwords are not displayed anywhere in the app.
- Verification and reset links are single-use and expire.
- Changing or resetting a password invalidates older application sessions.
- Never forward a password-reset or email-verification link.
- A collaboration link and a read-only snapshot link are access links; share them only with their intended recipients.
