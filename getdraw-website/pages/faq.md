# Frequently asked questions

## What is getdraw.app?

getdraw.app is an AI-assisted collaborative visual workspace for diagrams, whiteboards, wireframes, notes, and visual planning. The signed-in canvas is at `login.getdraw.app`.

## Do I need an account?

An account is required to save drawings, use AI features, collaborate in authorized drawing rooms, manage settings, and connect external agents. A recipient can open an exported read-only snapshot without signing in.

## What can I draw?

The editor includes shapes, sticky notes, text, images, free drawing, frames, arrows, lines, web embeds, and reusable libraries. It supports common styling, grouping, layering, alignment, search, themes, and portable exports.

## How does the Canvas assistant work?

It receives a structured description of the current canvas and conversation. It can create, connect, relabel, recolor, or remove supported objects. Select objects to focus its scope, or attach images and supported text files for additional context.

## Is AI output editable?

Yes. Canvas-assistant and flowchart generation paths create canvas elements with bound text and connectors. Some Mermaid types that the editor cannot convert may be inserted as images instead.

## Can I paste Mermaid?

Yes. The editor includes Mermaid preview and insertion. Its interactive converter supports several common diagram types. The MCP server's separate Mermaid tool currently accepts flowcharts only.

## Can Claude or ChatGPT create a drawing for me?

Yes, when the client supports remote MCP connections. Copy the MCP URL from Settings, complete sign-in and consent, and then ask the agent to create a drawing or flowchart. Exact connector setup varies by client.

## Can I revoke an agent connection?

Yes. Settings lists connected clients, scopes, last use, and expiry. Choose **Disconnect** to revoke one.

## Does getdraw.app support real-time collaboration?

Yes. Start a live room from a drawing, copy or share the link, show a QR code, and invite people by email. Authorized owners and editors can change the canvas; viewers remain read-only.

## What is the difference between collaboration and Export to Link?

Collaboration gives authorized participants a live room around the working drawing. Export to Link creates a separate encrypted, read-only snapshot that does not change when the original changes.

## Can I organize drawings?

Yes. Search, filter, sort, choose grid or list view, and move drawings into collections. The app also supports personal and available shared-workspace scopes.

## Can I recover a deleted drawing?

Owners can restore drawings from Trash for 90 days. **Delete forever** and **Empty trash** are permanent.

## What can I export?

You can save an editable `.excalidraw` file, export PNG or SVG, copy an image to the clipboard, or create a read-only share link. Supported image exports can embed scene data for later restoration.

## Does it work with a mouse and trackpad?

Yes. Mouse mode uses the wheel to zoom and right-drag to pan. Trackpad mode uses two-finger panning and pinch zooming. Auto mode detects the input per gesture.

## Is there a daily AI limit?

Yes. AI features use server-enforced daily allowances, and the generation interface reports remaining usage where available. The configured limit may vary by environment.

## Is getdraw.app based on Excalidraw?

Yes. The product builds on the open-source Excalidraw canvas and adds its own account, organization, AI, collaboration, sharing, settings, and agent-integration layers. The public site and legal pages should retain required open-source attribution.
