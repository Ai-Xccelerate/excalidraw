# Shared understanding happens on a shared canvas

Bring people into the same drawing, watch the work change live, and leave with an editable artifact the team can find again.

**CTA:** Start drawing together

## Collaborate in real time

Start a live room from any drawing. Copy the room link, use the device share sheet, or hand it to someone nearby with a QR code. Participants can see who is present and follow each other's location on the canvas.

## Invite people, not just browsers

Drawing owners can invite by email and review the member list. Invitations to people who have not signed up stay pending until they verify that address. Owners can remove a member or cancel a pending invitation.

Owner, editor, and viewer roles separate control from participation. Viewers can be present in the room without changing the scene.

## Choose live access or a frozen view

Not every review needs co-editing. Export an encrypted read-only snapshot when someone only needs the result. It opens without sign-in, does not grant access to the original drawing, and stays fixed even as the working canvas changes.

## Organize the work after the meeting

- Saved drawing titles, thumbnails, roles, and modified times
- Search, ownership filters, and chronological or alphabetical sorting
- Grid view for recognition; list view for detail
- Collections for projects, programs, or recurring workflows
- Personal and available shared-workspace scopes
- A separate Shared with you area for direct drawing access
- 90-day Trash recovery for owner-deleted drawings

## Share beyond getdraw.app

Export a portable editable file, PNG, SVG, or clipboard image. When supported, embed scene data in an image export to preserve a path back to editing.

## Current workspace note

The product supports shared workspace data and roles, and users can switch among workspaces already available to them. Workspace creation and workspace-member administration are not yet self-service controls in the current dashboard, so the initial public site should not promise a complete workspace-admin console.
