Editable by design

AI output you can keep working with.

Most AI image tools stop at the picture. getdraw.app creates diagram elements: labeled shapes, attached arrows, decision branches, and organized lanes.

  • Move a box without breaking its arrows.
  • Select a section and ask for a focused revision.
  • Change the words, style, route, or structure yourself.
  • Use Mermaid, plain language, or an external AI agent.
