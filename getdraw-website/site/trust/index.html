Access model

Participation without ambiguity.

Permission checks protect saved changes and live collaboration traffic.

OWNER

Control the drawing

Edit, invite, remove members, move to Trash, restore, and permanently delete.

EDITOR

Move the work

Edit, save, rename, and organize a drawing without receiving ownership controls.

VIEWER

Stay present

Open the drawing and participate in presence without sending canvas changes.

Account protection

Verified identity before invited access.

New accounts prove control of their email before they can sign in or claim an invitation.

01Single-use links

Email verification and password-reset tokens expire and cannot be replayed after use.

02Session invalidation

Changing or resetting a password invalidates older application sessions.

03Pending invitations

Access waits until the invited address is verified instead of trusting an unproven account.

Read-only sharing

Encrypted before the snapshot leaves the browser.

A shareable snapshot is encrypted client-side. Its decryption key lives in the link fragment rather than the snapshot storage request.

  • Opens without an account.
  • Remains separate from the original drawing.
  • Cannot be used to edit the account drawing.
  • Should still be shared intentionally: anyone with the full link can view it.

External AI agents

Explicit approval. Scoped access. Visible connections.

Compatible MCP clients connect through OAuth. The consent screen identifies the client and the access it requests.

Approve

Nothing is shared until you sign in and allow the request.

Limit

Scopes separate drawing reads, drawing creation, and visual preference access.

Disconnect

Review clients, last use, and expiry in Settings, then revoke access at any time.

Deleting is reversible first. Owner-deleted drawings stay recoverable for 90 days.
