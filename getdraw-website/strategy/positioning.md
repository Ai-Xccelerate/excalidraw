# Positioning and messaging

## Category

AI-assisted collaborative visual workspace.

## Positioning statement

For people and teams who need to explain systems, plan work, and turn rough thinking into clear visuals, getdraw.app is an infinite canvas where manual drawing, real-time collaboration, and AI diagramming work together. Unlike tools that generate a static picture or separate AI output from the source, getdraw.app produces editable canvas objects you can keep shaping, sharing, and organizing.

## Brand promise

From thought to shared visual, without breaking your flow.

## Message pillars

### 1. Draw naturally

Start with shapes, sticky notes, text, connectors, freehand drawing, images, frames, or Mermaid. The canvas adapts to mouse, trackpad, pen, and touch workflows.

**Benefit:** capture and clarify an idea at the speed it arrives.

### 2. Work with an AI drawing partner

Ask the Canvas assistant to create, connect, relabel, recolor, or reorganize the board. Give it the whole canvas, selected objects, an image, or a text attachment as context.

**Benefit:** move from blank canvas to usable structure quickly, then refine instead of restarting.

### 3. Keep the result editable

AI-created diagrams use canvas shapes, bound labels, and connected arrows. Agent-created flowcharts can include decisions, branch labels, and titled lanes.

**Benefit:** the output remains working material, not a dead image.

### 4. Think together

Invite people into a live drawing, share a QR code, manage drawing membership, or send a separate read-only snapshot.

**Benefit:** choose co-creation or presentation without confusing the two.

### 5. Keep visual work organized

Save drawings to an account, browse thumbnails, search, filter, sort, switch grid/list views, group work into collections, use workspace scopes, and recover deleted drawings for 90 days.

**Benefit:** the whiteboard becomes a durable body of work rather than a pile of browser tabs.

### 6. Let agents create where the work lives

Connect compatible AI clients through authenticated MCP. Approved agents can list owned drawings, read limited canvas text, apply saved visual defaults, and create new editable flowcharts or Mermaid-based drawings.

**Benefit:** a conversation in an AI client can end with a real drawing already waiting in the dashboard.

## Audience priorities

### Product and engineering teams

Architecture diagrams, data flows, incident maps, system boundaries, API journeys, and technical planning.

### Operators and business teams

Process maps, customer journeys, decision trees, service blueprints, operating models, and workshop boards.

### Designers and builders

Wireframes, flows, interface concepts, visual critiques, and HTML starting points generated from sketches.

### AI-native professionals

People who want Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or another compatible agent to create actual visual artifacts instead of only describing them.

## Voice

- Direct, active, and specific.
- Product-led rather than corporate.
- Confident about shipped behavior; explicit about limits.
- Prefer “editable diagram” over “AI-generated image.”
- Prefer “work together live” over vague collaboration language.
- Avoid “revolutionary,” “effortless,” “enterprise-grade,” and unprovable time-saved claims.
